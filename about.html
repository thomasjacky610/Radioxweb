About RadioX

We're an independent informational site. Here's what that means and what we're trying to do.

What This Site Is

RadioX is an independent guide to satellite radio. We write plainly about how the service works, what it offers, how much it costs, and which devices support it. That's it.

We're not a subscription seller, not a reseller, and not affiliated with any satellite radio provider. We don't get paid when you subscribe — we just put together the information people tend to search for and try to present it clearly.

Why We Built This

Most of the information available about satellite radio online is either from the provider itself (which has an obvious incentive to make everything sound great) or from tech review sites that cover it briefly as part of a roundup of every audio service available. Neither of these is particularly useful for someone who has a simple question like "is it actually worth paying for?" or "does my car have it built in?".

We wanted a resource that treats satellite radio as the single topic it is, gives you enough context to make an informed decision, and doesn't try to upsell you in the process.

Our Standards

We try to keep everything accurate, but satellite radio pricing, channel lineups, and plan names do change. We review our content periodically but can't guarantee every detail is current at any given moment. For anything consequential — like making a purchasing decision — always verify the details on the official provider website.

If you spot an error, we genuinely want to know. Use the contact form and we'll look into it.

Advertising

This site carries advertisements through Google's advertising program. Ads are labelled clearly and appear in designated slots on each page. The advertising doesn't influence our content — we don't take paid placement in articles and don't write sponsored posts.
