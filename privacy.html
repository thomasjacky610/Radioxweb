Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

RadioX ("we", "us") is an independent informational website. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit the site and how we use it.

What We Collect

  • Contact form submissions — your name, email, and message when you use the contact form
  • Usage data — pages visited, time on site, browser type, and device information collected via analytics software
  • Cookies — small files placed on your device by the website and our advertising partner

We don't collect payment information, passwords, or sensitive personal data.

How We Use It

  • To respond to messages sent through the contact form
  • To understand how visitors use the site so we can improve it
  • To display relevant advertisements through our advertising partner

Cookies & Advertising

We use analytics software and display advertising through an advertising partner. The advertising partner may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalised advertising through your browser settings or your advertising preferences page.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with our hosting provider and advertising partner, both of which process it on our behalf under appropriate agreements.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing privacy@xmactive.com. We respond within 30 days.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use of the site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.
