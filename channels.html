Music Channels

Music is the heart of satellite radio and it's where the commercial-free promise really matters. Unlike FM where you might hear 10 minutes of ads every half hour, music channels on satellite radio run continuously without interruption.

The lineup covers virtually every genre imaginable, with dedicated channels for specific eras and sub-genres. A few examples of what you'll find:

  • Classic hits from the 1950s through the 1990s, each decade with its own channel
  • Contemporary pop, rock, hip-hop, and R&B
  • Country music — both mainstream hits and deeper cuts
  • Jazz, blues, classical, and instrumental
  • Electronic, dance, and alternative formats
  • Latin, reggae, world music, and gospel
  • Children's music for family listening

Many channels are curated by genre specialists rather than algorithm, which tends to make them feel more like a radio show than a shuffle playlist.

The channel guide is easy to browse by genre or format.

Sports Coverage

Live sports is probably the second biggest reason people subscribe. Local FM radio covers local teams. Satellite radio covers every team in every major league — home and away broadcasts included.

🏈

Professional Football

Every regular season and playoff game. Home and away broadcasts so you hear your team's local announcers no matter where you're listening from.

⚾

Professional Baseball

Complete coverage of every game throughout the regular season and postseason, with both home and away broadcast teams available.

🏀

Professional Basketball

Full season coverage with live play-by-play, halftime analysis, and post-game shows. Every team's games are available.

🏒

Professional Hockey

Regular season and championship coverage with home and away broadcasts throughout the full season.

🏁

Auto Racing

Live race broadcasts, in-car audio from the drivers, and expert commentary throughout the racing season.

🎓

College Sports

Major conference football and basketball, bowl games, and tournament coverage that doesn't make it to local radio.

News & Talk

The news offering on satellite radio is genuinely useful if you spend a lot of time in the car. Major national news networks broadcast live 24 hours a day, which means you're not limited to the local station's brief hourly updates.

The talk category is broader — you'll find political discussion from across the spectrum, business news, health and lifestyle programming, and entertainment coverage. Some of the most popular satellite radio programming falls in this category, including exclusive celebrity-hosted shows that aren't available anywhere else.

Comedy & Entertainment

There are several channels dedicated to stand-up comedy — classic performances from well-known comedians and newer acts — as well as comedy talk formats. These channels are particularly good for long drives when you want something engaging but not as demanding as news or sports.

Entertainment channels cover celebrity interviews, pop culture discussion, film and TV commentary, and variety programming. The content here changes more frequently than music channels, which keeps it fresh.

Heads up: The specific channel lineup available to you depends on your subscription plan. Music and most talk channels are included across all plans, but some premium and exclusive channels require a higher-tier subscription. See the plans page for a full breakdown.
